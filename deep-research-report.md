**Credit Transfer Explanation:** Credit transfer (also called recognition of prior learning) lets students count previously earned credits when moving to a new course. In the UK, credit frameworks (e.g. SCQF) allow, for example, an HND holder to enter the final year of a degree program, transferring ~240 credits【22†L1-L4】【23†L15-L21】. In Nepal, formal credit transfer systems are not well-established; credits earned in one program (like a CTEVT diploma) do not automatically transfer to a university degree, although some institutions may consider partial exemption on a case-by-case basis【24†L9-L13】. We found no official “credit transfer arrangement” offered for EECOHM’s courses.
